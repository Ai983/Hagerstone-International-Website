## About Focal Point

Ludhiana's Focal Point estates are the city's main organised industrial areas,
developed by PSIEC to house the manufacturing that makes Ludhiana Punjab's
industrial capital — engineering goods, auto components, textiles and hosiery,
and pharma. Most projects are factory buildings, extensions, warehouses and the
admin blocks that serve them.

## What projects here need

- **Buildings designed around the process** — floor loads, clear heights and
  utilities matched to the machinery.
- **Extensions on running plants** — work sequenced around production.
- **Compliance-ready admin blocks** — offices, QA and canteens delivered to the
  same standard as the plant.
