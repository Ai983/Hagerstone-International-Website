## About Peenya

Peenya in north-west Bengaluru is one of the largest industrial areas in South
Asia, developed largely by KIADB and home to engineering, machine-tool,
electronics and precision manufacturing units. Projects here are factories,
extensions, plant offices and building services upgrades.

## What projects here need

- **Precision-manufacturing environments** — clean, well-lit, well-ventilated
  production and assembly areas.
- **Upgrades on running facilities** — work phased around production.
- **Integrated services** — electrical, fire protection and HVAC delivered with
  the building.
