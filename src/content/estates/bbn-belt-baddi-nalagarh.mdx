## About the BBN belt

The Baddi-Barotiwala-Nalagarh belt in Himachal Pradesh's Solan district grew on
the back of industrial incentives into India's largest pharmaceutical
manufacturing cluster. Plants here need corporate offices, QA and admin blocks,
canteens and plant building services alongside their production facilities.

## What projects here need

- **Delivery into a remote, hill-adjacent location** — mobilisation and supply
  planned for distance from the NCR.
- **Pharma-standard discipline** — clean execution and documentation even on
  non-process buildings.
- **Work beside running plants** — sequencing around production.
