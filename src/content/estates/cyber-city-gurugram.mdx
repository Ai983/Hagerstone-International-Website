## About Cyber City

DLF Cyber City is Gurugram's densest corporate district — a cluster of IT and
office towers, managed workspaces and supporting retail next to NH-48 and the
Rapid Metro. Tenants include large technology, consulting and multinational
firms, and floors change hands regularly as leases turn over.

That turnover defines the work: tenant fit-outs on Cat A floors, facade
refurbishment on buildings that are now two decades old, and building services
rework each time a floor is re-planned.

## What projects here need

- **Programmes tied to lease dates** — late handover costs the tenant rent on two
  offices.
- **Work in occupied buildings** — facade and MEP work around live tenants, with
  controlled access, noise windows and safety at height.
- **Re-cladding older blocks** — upgrading envelopes installed years ago to
  current fire and thermal expectations.
