## About Aerocity

Delhi Aerocity is a hospitality and commercial district developed within the IGI
Airport precinct, combining international hotels with office buildings for
companies that value airport access. Projects here are premium office fit-outs
and hospitality interiors, delivered to a high finish standard on managed,
access-controlled sites.
