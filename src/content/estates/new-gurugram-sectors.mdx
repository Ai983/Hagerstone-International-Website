## About these sectors

The Golf Course Extension Road, New Gurugram and the Dwarka Expressway corridor
are where most of Gurugram's new premium residential and mixed-use towers have
been built over the last decade. The work here is dominated by high-rise
fenestration, balcony railings, podium glazing and facades for large developers.

## What projects here need

- **Repetition at quality** — hundreds of identical openings that must all perform.
- **Wind loading at height** — upper-floor fenestration and railings are designed
  for the building's wind pressure, not a standard detail.
- **Pace matched to the structure** — installation that keeps up with a tower
  going up floor by floor.
