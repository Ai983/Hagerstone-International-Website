## About Sector 63

Noida's Sectors 57 to 65 form a mixed corridor of IT offices, electronics
manufacturing, industrial units and commercial showrooms, close to the Sector 62
IT hub and the NH-24 approach. Buildings range from industrial units being
converted to offices to purpose-built showrooms and corporate floors.
