## About Saket

Saket is a major South Delhi district centre combining large hospitals, shopping
malls and commercial offices within a compact area. Projects here are typically
upgrades and extensions on operating sites — hospitals that cannot close, malls
that keep trading, and offices re-fitted between tenants.

## What projects here need

- **Work on live, high-footfall sites** — hospitals and malls that stay open.
- **Constrained logistics** — limited access and storage in a dense district.
- **Healthcare-grade discipline** where the building is clinical.
