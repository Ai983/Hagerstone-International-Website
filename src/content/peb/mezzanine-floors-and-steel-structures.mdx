## What we deliver

**Steel mezzanine floors, platforms, walkways, staircases and multi-level steel
structures** for factories, warehouses, showrooms and commercial buildings —
designed, fabricated and erected, including in existing buildings.

A mezzanine is the fastest way to get more usable floor inside an existing
building. It is also a structure that people and goods stand on, which makes the
load case and the escape route the two things to get right before anything else.

| Finishing | Deck surface, fire protection where required |

## Where projects go wrong

- **Designed for office load, used for storage.** A common and dangerous
  mismatch.
- **Existing slab not checked.** Mezzanine columns bring concentrated loads down
  onto a floor that was not designed for them.
- **Escape routes not considered.** Added floor area needs exits.
- **Clearances overlooked.** Headroom under and over the mezzanine, and around
  services and sprinklers.
- **No edge protection.** Open edges need handrails and toe boards.
