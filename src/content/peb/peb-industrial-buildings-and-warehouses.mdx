## What we deliver

**Pre-engineered factories, warehouses, logistics sheds and industrial
buildings** — structure, cladding, civil foundations and flooring, and MEP
delivered together rather than split between a steel supplier and several site
contractors.

An industrial building is judged on a few things: clear span, clear height,
floor capacity, and how soon it can start operating. PEB is chosen because it
wins on all four — provided the foundations, the steel and the services are ready
for each other.

## What drives the cost

Clear span and eave height · crane capacity · roof and wall cladding
specification · insulation · floor loading and slab thickness · number of dock
openings · fire protection required for the stored goods · site access for
steel delivery and cranes.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Steel arrives before foundations are ready.** Erection stalls and the steel
  sits in the open.
- **Grade slab designed without racking loads.** High-bay racking concentrates
  large point loads on the floor.
- **Fire requirements set after the design.** Sprinklers and hydrants change the
  roof height, water storage and pump room.
- **Ventilation and daylighting ignored.** Large sheds without them are hot and
  dark and need lighting all day.
- **Drainage undersized.** A large roof collects a great deal of water in a
  monsoon downpour.
