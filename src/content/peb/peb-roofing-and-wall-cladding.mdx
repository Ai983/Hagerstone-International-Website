  - question: "Single-skin sheeting or insulated panels?"
    answer: "Single-skin metal sheeting is the economical choice for storage sheds where temperature does not matter much. Insulated sandwich panels — PIR or rock wool between two metal skins — cost more but cut heat gain dramatically, which matters for any building with people working inside, temperature-sensitive goods, or air conditioning.   - question: "Why do industrial roofs leak?"
    answer: "Mostly at fasteners, laps and penetrations rather than through the sheet itself. Over-tightened or under-tightened screws, side laps not sealed, end laps too short for the roof slope, and poorly flashed penetrations for ventilators and pipes account for most leaks. They are installation issues, and they are avoided by detailing and supervision rather than a better sheet."

## What we deliver

**Roof and wall sheeting, insulated sandwich panels, skylights, ventilators,
flashings and gutters** for PEB and industrial buildings — the envelope that
decides how hot the building runs and whether it stays dry.

On an industrial building the envelope is most of the building's surface area,
and the roof takes the most sun. Its specification sets the inside temperature,
the lighting load and the running cost for as long as the building stands.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Fastener spacing uniform across the roof.** Edges and corners need closer
  spacing for wind uplift.
- **Laps not sealed or too short.** The most common cause of industrial roof
  leaks.
- **Penetrations poorly flashed.** Every ventilator and pipe is a potential leak.
- **Skylights without insulation strategy.** They bring daylight and heat.
- **Gutters undersized.** A large roof sheds a great deal of water quickly.
