## What hospitality clients need

Hotels and serviced apartments are judged by guests at arm's length and run by
staff behind the scenes. That means premium, durable finishes in guest areas,
back-of-house that supports operations, services that are quiet and reliable,
and — on renovations — a method that keeps the property trading.
