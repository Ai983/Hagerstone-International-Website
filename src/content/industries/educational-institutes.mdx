## What educational clients need

Schools, colleges and training centres are dense, heavily used and tied to the
academic calendar. Buildings must be safe for large numbers of occupants,
durable under daily wear, and comfortable enough — in light, air and sound — to
learn in. Most upgrade work has to fit inside vacations.
