## What pharma clients need

Pharma sites are regulated, documented and schedule-driven. Even the buildings
around the production process — admin blocks, QA offices, canteens, corporate
floors — are expected to be delivered with the same discipline: clean
execution, controlled documentation and minimal disruption to a running plant.
