## What healthcare clients need

In a hospital, building services are clinical infrastructure. Power continuity
in an ICU, air movement in an operation theatre and water quality in sterile
areas all have direct consequences for patients. Finishes must be cleanable and
durable, circulation must support patient flow, and much of the work happens
while the hospital stays open.
