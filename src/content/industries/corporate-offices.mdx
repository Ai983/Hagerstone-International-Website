## What corporate clients need

Corporate offices are driven by dates and by people. The lease start, the notice
period on the old office and the move plan fix the handover date; after that, the
office is judged on how it feels to work in every day.

That puts two things at the centre of every corporate project: a programme that
holds, and the building services — cooling, light, acoustics, power and data —
that decide daily comfort.
