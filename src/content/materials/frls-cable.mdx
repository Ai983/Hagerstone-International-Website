## What it is

A family of cable specifications defined by **how the cable behaves in a
fire**, rather than by its electrical properties.

Three distinct things get confused in Indian specifications:

| Type | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **FRLS** | Resists flame spread, reduces smoke and acid gas while burning |
| **LSZH** | As above, with zero halogen content |
| **Fire survival** | Keeps the circuit energised during a fire for a stated period |

**FRLS is not fire resistant.** It burns; it just burns less badly.

## Why smoke is the specification that saves lives

Most fire deaths are from smoke inhalation, not burns. A standard PVC cable in
a burning shaft produces dense black smoke and hydrogen chloride gas, which
both obscures escape routes and attacks the lungs. In a stair core or a
basement, that is the mechanism that kills.

Reducing smoke and acid gas from the very cables that run through escape routes
is therefore a life safety measure, not a specification upgrade.

## Mapping cable type to location

| Location or duty | Typical requirement |
| --- | --- |
| General distribution in open areas | FRLS |
| Escape routes, stairs, lobbies | LSZH preferred |
| Basements and enclosed shafts | LSZH |
| Fire pumps, pressurisation fans | **Fire survival** |
| Fire alarm and emergency lighting | **Fire survival** |
| Data centres and critical rooms | LSZH |

## Where projects go wrong

- **FRLS specified for fire pumps.** The pump stops when the cable burns
  through — exactly when it is needed.
- **One grade across the whole project**, either overspending everywhere or
  underspecifying in the places that matter.
- **Fire-survival cable installed with ordinary accessories.** The rating
  belongs to the complete system, including glands, clips and supports; plastic
  clips melt and drop the cable.
- **Certificates not verified** against the delivered drum.

## Standards referenced

Fire and life safety provisions for services under **NBC 2016, Part 4**. Cable
