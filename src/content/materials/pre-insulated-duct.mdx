  - question: "What does pre-insulated duct actually save?"
    answer: "Height, weight and time. The insulation is the duct wall rather than a wrap applied afterwards, so the overall section is smaller for the same free area — which matters when the ceiling void is tight and services are stacked. It is a fraction of the weight of GI, so hanger loads drop, and it is one trade and one operation instead of fabricate, install, then insulate."
  - question: "Is it acceptable on fire safety grounds?"
    answer: "It depends on the product and where it runs. The panels are aluminium faced with a PIR core, so the core is combustible even though the facings are not. Products have a named EN 13501-1 classification and that class must be checked against the application, particularly in escape routes, shafts and high-occupancy spaces. It is not a substitute for a fire-rated duct where one is required."

## What it is

Ductwork made from **rigid PIR panel faced with aluminium on both sides**, cut
and folded into sections on site. The insulation and the duct are the same
component.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Panel | PIR core, aluminium facings both sides |
| Panel thickness | 20mm — 30mm typical |
| Facing | Aluminium foil, thickness stated |
| Standard | EN 13403 for non-metallic ducts |
| Fire class | Named EN 13501-1 classification |
| Pressure limit | Stated by manufacturer; not unlimited |

## Against GI ductwork

| | Pre-insulated PIR | GI with wrap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Void height needed | Less | More |
| Weight | Much lighter | Heavy |
| Install sequence | One operation | Fabricate, fit, then insulate |
| Robustness | Lower; damages easily | High |
| Pressure capability | Limited | Wide range |
| Outdoor use | Needs weather jacket | Standard |

## Where it earns its place

Tight ceiling voids in fit-outs where the slab-to-slab height is fixed and
services must be stacked · refurbishments where structural hanger capacity is
limited · fast-track programmes where removing the separate insulation trade
saves real days · low and medium pressure comfort cooling in office space.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Used in the wrong location** — kitchen extract, exposed plant rooms, or
  outdoors without a jacket.
- **Fire class not checked** against the route the duct takes.
- **Joints poorly made.** The system relies on correct corner profiles and
  taping; a rushed joint leaks and the aluminium facing tears.
- **Hangers over-tightened**, crushing the panel and reducing the free area.
- **Pressure class exceeded**, causing panel deflection and joint failure.

## Standards referenced

Non-metallic duct requirements under **EN 13403**. Reaction-to-fire
classification to **EN 13501-1**. Duct insulation requirements under
**ECBC 2017**.
