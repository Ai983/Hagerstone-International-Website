## What it is

The **structural backbone of an aluminium facade**: alloy 6063 in T6 temper,
extruded into hollow and open profiles that carry glass, resist wind and hold
the gaskets, hardware and drainage that make a window work.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Alloy and temper | 6063-T6 to IS 733 |
| Ultimate tensile strength | Approximately 215 MPa |
| Wall thickness | Stated explicitly, typically 1.4mm — 3.0mm |
| Anodising | Grade to IS 1868, thickness in microns |
| Powder coat | Resin class named; AAMA 2605 for PVDF |
| Straightness and twist | Tolerance stated on the order |

## What to verify on a supply

- **Wall thickness against the approved section**, measured, not assumed
- **Anodic coating thickness in microns** — commonly 15 micron internal,
  20–25 micron external, higher for coastal exposure
- **Powder coat resin class** on the certificate, not the colour name
- **Temper certificate** for the delivered batch
- **Screw port dimensions**, since hardware retention depends on them

## Why the finish class decides service life

A polyester powder coat and a PVDF powder coat look identical on handover. On a
south or west elevation in India, the polyester chalks and fades within a few
years while the PVDF holds colour for well over a decade. The price difference
at supply is modest; the difference in when the building needs recoating is
enormous, and recoating an installed facade is scaffolding, access and
disruption.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Wall thickness unstated**, so the cheapest quote wins on a thinner section
  that deflects more and holds hardware worse.
- **"Powder coated" with no resin class.** Unenforceable.
- **Anodising thickness unspecified** for a coastal site.
- **Mixing profile families** across one facade, so gaskets and hardware no
  longer match the tested assembly.
- **Dissimilar metal contact** — steel fixings directly against aluminium
  without isolation set up galvanic corrosion at the interface.

## Standards referenced

Alloy and temper under **IS 733**. Anodic coating grades under **IS 1868**.
High-performance organic coating performance to **AAMA 2605**.
