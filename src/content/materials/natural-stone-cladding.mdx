## What it is

**Dimension stone** — granite, sandstone, limestone, marble or slate — cut into
slabs and hung on a facade with stainless steel anchors in a drained,
ventilated cavity.

Indian projects have exceptional access to domestic granite and sandstone,
which is why stone remains commercially viable here at scales that would be
prohibitive elsewhere.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Stone | Identified to IS 1123, named quarry and batch |
| Thickness | 25mm — 40mm typical, by calculation |
| Finish | Honed, flamed, bush-hammered, polished |
| Anchors | Stainless steel, grade by exposure |
| Fixing | Kerf, undercut or dowel, into a drained cavity |
| Testing | Flexural, compressive, absorption, pull-out |

## The anchor is the design, not the stone

A stone facade fails at the anchor, not through the slab. Everything that
matters structurally is in the fixing:

- **Anchor grade.** 304 stainless pits in coastal and heavily polluted air;
  316 is the grade for those environments.
- **Edge distance and kerf depth** control whether the anchor pulls through the
  slab edge.
- **Pull-out testing on the supplied stone** is the only evidence the anchor
  capacity is real.
- **Each slab independently supported**, so a single failure is not
  progressive.

## What drives the cost

Stone type and rarity · slab thickness · finish · panel size and yield from the
block · anchor type, with undercut costing more than kerf · wastage, which is
high for stone · testing programme · handling, weight and crane time.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Thickness copied from another project.** Wind pressure and stone strength
  are both project-specific.
- **Test values taken from quarry literature** rather than the supplied batch.
- **304 stainless near the coast.** It stains and pits within a few seasons.
- **Wet fixing above low rise.** Mortar adhesion is not a facade support system.
- **No breakage allowance.** Stone chips and cracks in transit; the order must
  carry margin.

## Standards referenced

Stone identification under **IS 1123**. Compressive strength testing to
**ASTM C170**. Wind loading on cladding and anchors under **IS 875 (Part 3)**.
