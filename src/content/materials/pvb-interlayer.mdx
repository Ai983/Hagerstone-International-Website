## What it is

**Polyvinyl butyral**, a tough plastic interlayer bonded between glass panes
under heat and pressure. When the glass breaks the interlayer holds the
fragments in place, which is what makes laminated glass a safety product.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Ply thickness | 0.38mm increments |
| Common builds | 0.38 / **0.76** / 1.52mm |
| Grades | Standard, acoustic, UV-blocking, coloured |
| Standard | EN ISO 12543, conformity to EN 14449 |
| Safety | To IS 2553 (Part 1) |
| UV block | Over 99% in standard grades |

## Why the interlayer is the specification

Two laminated panes with identical glass can behave completely differently:

- **0.38mm** — basic fragment retention
- **0.76mm** — the workhorse for safety glazing, barriers and overhead
- **1.52mm and above** — higher impact, security and acoustic duty
- **Acoustic grade** — measurably better sound damping at traffic frequencies
- **Ionoplast** — a different polymer entirely, far stiffer, for structural use

Naming only "laminated" gets you the cheapest of these.

## Where it matters most

**Barriers and balustrades.** If a toughened pane in a barrier fails, the
interlayer is the only thing keeping the barrier standing. This is a life
safety function, not a finish.

**Overhead and skylight glazing**, where a failed pane must not fall.

**Acoustic facades** on road-facing elevations.

**UV protection** for retail displays and interiors, where PVB blocks nearly
all UV and limits fading of finishes and merchandise.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Interlayer thickness unstated.**
- **Standing water at the pane edge** from an undrained rebate, causing
  delamination.
- **Standard PVB where acoustic was intended** — visually identical, acoustically
  not.
- **Cutting laminated glass on site.** It cannot be cut cleanly after
  lamination; sizes must be right at order stage.

## Standards referenced

Laminated glass constituents under **EN ISO 12543**, conformity under
**EN 14449**, and architectural safety glass under **IS 2553 (Part 1)**.
