## What it is

**Cold-rolled steel sheet coated with an aluminium-zinc alloy**, nominally 55%
aluminium, 43.5% zinc and 1.5% silicon. Supplied bare, or pre-painted as PPGL
for a coloured finish.

It is the default substrate for industrial roofing and wall cladding across
Indian PEB construction.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | IS 15961 or ASTM A792 |
| Coating mass | AZ70 / AZ100 / **AZ150** and above |
| Base steel yield | 345 MPa and 550 MPa grades common |
| Thickness | 0.45mm — 0.80mm TCT for roofing |
| Finish | Bare Galvalume, or pre-painted PPGL |
| Paint system | Polyester, SMP or PVDF by exposure |

**TCT** means total coated thickness — always confirm whether a quoted
thickness is TCT or base metal thickness, because the difference is real money.

## Why coating mass is the specification that matters

Two sheets can both be "Galvalume" and differ in service life by many years.
The variable is coating mass:

| Coating | Indicative use |
| --- | --- |
| AZ70 | Sheltered, low-exposure, short-life structures |
| AZ100 | General inland industrial |
| AZ150 | Standard for durable industrial roofing |
| AZ200 and above | Coastal, chemically aggressive, long-design-life |

## What drives the cost

Base metal thickness · coating mass · steel grade · bare versus pre-painted ·
paint resin class, with PVDF above SMP above polyester · profile and rib depth ·
sheet length and transport.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Coating mass unspecified.** The most common and most expensive omission.
- **Thickness quoted as TCT and read as base metal.** A 0.50mm TCT sheet is
  thinner in steel than a 0.50mm base metal sheet.
- **Cut edges and swarf left on the roof.** Drilling swarf rusts in place and
  stains and pits the sheet. It must be swept at the end of every shift.
- **Contact with wet concrete, copper or lead.** Each drives accelerated
  corrosion; isolate the interfaces.
- **Fastener spacing uniform across the roof.** Edges, corners and ridges see
  higher suction under IS 875 Part 3 and need closer spacing.

## Standards referenced

Aluminium-zinc coated sheet under **IS 15961** and **ASTM A792**. Wind loading
on roof and wall cladding to **IS 875 (Part 3)**.
