## What it is

Float glass whose surface has been **chemically etched** to a uniform satin
finish. The result diffuses light evenly while obscuring detail behind it.

It is the default privacy glass in commercial interiors.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Process | Chemical etch, one or both faces |
| Finish | Uniform satin; no directional grain |
| Grades | Standard, and anti-fingerprint / easy-clean |
| Toughening | Etch first, then toughen to EN 12150 |
| Safety | To IS 2553 (Part 1) where a barrier or door |
| Thickness | 6mm — 12mm typical for partitions |

## Where it is used

Office front and meeting room partitions · door leaves and side panels ·
washroom and changing area screens · balustrade infill where privacy is wanted
· feature walls and signage backers · shelving and light diffusers.

## Etched against the alternatives

| Option | Cleanability | Uniformity | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acid etched | Good; better in easy-clean grade | Excellent | Moderate |
| Sandblasted | Poor; holds grease and marks | Variable | Lower |
| Applied film | Good until it lifts or scratches | Good | Lowest |
| Ceramic frit | Excellent | Excellent | Higher |

Film is cheapest and is what gets substituted when budget tightens. It scratches,
edges lift, and it is visibly a film at close range — which in a lobby or a
director's office is exactly where it is seen.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Sandblasted substituted for etched** on a touched surface. It never looks
  clean again.
- **Standard grade where hands touch constantly.** Specify the anti-fingerprint
  grade on doors and office fronts.
- **Privacy assumed rather than sampled.** Look at a real sample at real
  distance under the real lighting before approving.
- **Manifestation forgotten.** A full-height satin panel still needs a
  visibility marking where people might walk into it.

## Standards referenced

Architectural safety glass under **IS 2553 (Part 1)**. Thermally toughened
safety glass to **EN 12150**.
