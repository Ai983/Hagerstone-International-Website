## What it is

**Ethylene propylene diene monomer rubber**, extruded into profiles that seal
glass to frame and frame to frame. It is the sealing element in almost every
engineered aluminium fenestration and curtain wall system.

Small item, disproportionate consequence.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Material | EPDM, dense or sponge by function |
| Hardness | 60 — 70 Shore A typical for glazing |
| Classification | ASTM D2000 line call-out |
| Corners | Vulcanised, not cut and butted |
| Colour | Black standard; grey and white available |
| Service life | Decades under UV without embrittlement |

## Why it outlasts the alternatives

EPDM's polymer backbone has no unsaturated bonds in the main chain, which is
the technical reason it resists ozone and UV attack where other elastomers
harden. In practical terms: an EPDM gasket on a west elevation in Delhi is
still compressible after twenty summers. A PVC gasket in the same position is
often hard and shrunken within five.

A hardened gasket does not seal. Water then enters the glazing rebate, attacks
the DGU secondary seal, and the unit fogs.

## Where it sits in the system

| Location | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Glazing rebate, inner and outer | Seals glass to frame, allows movement |
| Frame to frame joints | Seals the assembly line |
| Drainage path | Shaped to keep the rebate drained, not dammed |
| Compression seal on openable sashes | Air and water tightness when closed |

## Where projects go wrong

- **Cut and butted corners** on a system that was tested with vulcanised ones.
- **Gasket substitution** at supply stage, voiding the tested performance.
- **Over-compression**, which takes a permanent set and leaves the seal slack.
- **Stretching gaskets during installation.** A stretched gasket recovers over
  months and pulls back from the corners, opening a gap at exactly the point
  water collects.
- **Blocked drainage.** A gasket seated wrongly can dam the rebate instead of
  draining it.

## Standards referenced

Rubber material classification under **ASTM D2000**. Assembly air leakage to
**ASTM E283** and water penetration to **ASTM E331**, both of which are tested
with the specified gasket in place.
