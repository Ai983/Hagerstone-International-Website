## What it is

A sheet of **cement, cellulose fibre and mineral fillers**, cured and pressed to
a defined density. Facade grades are through-coloured or factory-coated;
substrate grades are left plain for tiling or painting.

It occupies the space between a decorative panel and a structural sheathing
board, and is specified for both.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | EN 12467, category and class named |
| Thickness | 6mm — 12mm facade; 6mm — 20mm substrate |
| Fire class | A2-s1,d0 or A1 to EN 13501-1 |
| Density | Approximately 1,300 — 1,800 kg/m³ |
| Finish | Through-coloured, factory coated, or plain |
| Fixing | Visible screw or rivet; rear rail systems available |

## Why it gets specified

**Fire class, on a decorative panel.** Very few materials give a coloured
rainscreen face at A2 or A1. On high-rise and high-occupancy buildings this
removes the core-grade argument entirely.

**Dimensional stability.** Movement is small compared with HPL or timber, so
joint and fixing detailing is less demanding.

**Dual use.** The same material family gives you a facade panel and the tile
substrate in the wet areas inside, which simplifies procurement on a
design-and-build package.

## Where it is used

| Application | Grade |
| --- | --- |
| Ventilated rainscreen facade | Through-coloured or coated, 8mm+ |
| Tile substrate in showers and wet pantries | Plain substrate board |
| Soffits and canopy linings | Coated facade grade |
| Sheathing behind rainscreen | Plain, higher thickness |
| Fire-rated shaft and duct enclosure | Specified board with tested system |

## What drives the cost

Density grade · thickness · through-coloured versus coated versus plain · panel
size and cutting waste · fixing method · edge sealing requirements · whether
the board forms part of a tested fire-rated system, which restricts substitution.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Cut edges left unsealed** on a facade. The cut edge is the absorbent face;
  it must be sealed with the manufacturer's product or it weathers unevenly.
- **Substrate grade used as a finish.** Plain board is not a facade panel and
  will not hold appearance outdoors.
- **Drilling without the right bit.** The board dulls standard bits quickly and
  chips at the face; carbide tooling and correct hole sizes matter.
- **Treating it as waterproof** in wet areas. It is water-resistant; the
  membrane is still required.

## Standards referenced

Flat sheet product specification under **EN 12467**. Reaction-to-fire
classification to **EN 13501-1**. Fire and life safety provisions for external
walls under **NBC 2016, Part 4**.
