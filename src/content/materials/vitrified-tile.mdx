## What it is

**Pressed ceramic tile fired to low porosity**, where the body vitrifies into a
dense, largely impervious mass. The standard hard floor finish in Indian
commercial and residential construction.

## The types

| Type | Wear surface | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full body** | Through the thickness | Heavy traffic, industrial, commercial |
| **Double charge** | Thick pigment layer | General commercial, corridors |
| **Glazed (GVT)** | Printed glaze | Design-led areas, moderate traffic |
| **Polished** | Polished surface | Dry lobbies only — slip risk |

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | IS 15622, or EN 14411 group BIa |
| Water absorption | Below 0.5% |
| Slip rating | R-class stated for wet areas |
| Format | Up to 1200 × 2400mm in large formats |
| Rectification | Rectified edges for narrow joints |
| Thickness | 8mm — 12mm typical for floors |

## What the specification must state

Tile type, not just size and colour · water absorption class · **slip rating
per area** · rectified or non-rectified edges · joint width, since rectified
tiles allow narrow joints but demand a flatter substrate · adhesive class and
coverage requirement · movement joint positions.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Polished tile at entrances.** It is dangerous when wet.
- **GVT in heavy traffic**, wearing through the glaze in lanes.
- **Dab-fixed large formats**, leaving voids that crack under point loads.
- **Movement joints omitted** across large floor areas, so tiles tent and pop.
- **Batch and shade variation** not checked before laying.

## Standards referenced

Pressed ceramic tile specification under **IS 15622** and **EN 14411**. Slip
resistance classification per **DIN 51130** practice.
