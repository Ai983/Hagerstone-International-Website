  - question: "Glass wool or rock wool — which should be used?"
    answer: "Glass wool is lighter, cheaper and slightly better per millimetre thermally, so it suits duct wrap, ceiling plenum blankets and partition infill. Rock wool withstands far higher temperatures and holds its form better at density, so it is required for cavity barriers, ventilated facade slabs and high-temperature plant.   - question: "What facing should duct insulation have?"

## What it is

**Mineral fibre spun from molten glass**, bound into rolls, slabs and pipe
sections. Lighter and cheaper than rock wool, with slightly better thermal
conductivity per millimetre and lower temperature tolerance.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | IS 8183, bonded mineral wool |
| Thermal conductivity | Approximately 0.032 — 0.040 W/mK |
| Density range | 12 — 48 kg/m³ typical |
| Fire class | A1 / A2 to EN 13501-1 by product and facing |
| Facing | Plain, reinforced foil, tissue, or black glass tissue |
| Service temperature | Lower than rock wool |

## Density by application

| Application | Density |
| --- | --- |
| Duct wrap, external | 16 — 24 kg/m³, foil faced |
| Duct lining, internal acoustic | 32 — 48 kg/m³, tissue faced |
| Ceiling plenum blanket | 12 — 16 kg/m³ |
| Partition acoustic infill | 24 — 48 kg/m³ |
| Pipe section | Preformed, density by diameter |

## Where it belongs on a fit-out

**Duct wrap** on conditioned-air and chilled-water ducts, foil faced for vapour
control. **Internal duct lining** where fan and airflow noise must be absorbed
before it reaches the occupied space. **Partition infill** between meeting
rooms and tenancies, where the acoustic rating of the partition depends on it.
**Above ceiling**, as a plenum blanket where the slab soffit is uninsulated.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Unfaced insulation on a cold duct.** Condensation forms inside the
  insulation, performance collapses, and ceilings stain below.
- **Foil joints untaped.** The vapour barrier is only continuous if the laps
  are sealed; an untaped lap is an open path.
- **Insulation compressed by a cable tray or hanger.** Compression reduces
  thickness and performance at exactly the points nobody inspects.
- **Glass wool used as a cavity barrier.** It is not the specified material for
  that duty; rock wool is.

## Standards referenced

Bonded mineral wool under **IS 8183**. Reaction-to-fire classification to
**EN 13501-1**. Services and envelope insulation requirements under
**ECBC 2017**.
