A fire-smoke damper closes on a signal from the fire alarm or smoke detection system, so it acts on smoke long before temperatures rise. ## What it is

A **closure device inside ductwork** at the point it crosses a fire compartment
boundary, restoring the fire resistance of the wall or floor it passes through.

## Types

| Type | Actuation | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fire damper** | Fusible link, heat | General compartment crossings |
| **Fire-smoke damper** | Motorised, alarm signal | Escape routes, pressurisation |
| **Smoke damper** | Motorised, detection | Smoke control systems |
| **Ceiling radiation damper** | Heat | Ceiling penetrations in rated floors |

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Rating | Matches the wall or floor rating — 60, 120 minutes |
| Standard | EN 15650 or UL 555 |
| Link temperature | Commonly 72°C for fire dampers |
| Frame | Purpose-made installation frame, built into the wall |
| Actuator | Spring-return on motorised types |
| Access | Panel sized and positioned to reach the damper |

## Installation is where the rating lives

A correctly rated damper installed badly gives no protection at all:

- The damper must be **built into the wall** on its installation frame, not
  hung off the duct beside it
- The gap around the frame must be fire-stopped with a **tested** system
- The duct must be able to fall away in a fire without pulling the damper out
- An **access panel** must reach the damper for testing and resetting
- Links and blades must be **clear of paint** and free to move

## Where projects go wrong

- **No access panel.** The damper can never be tested or reset.
- **Fixed to the duct**, not the wall.
- **Fire stopping done with general-purpose sealant** rather than a tested
  system.
- **Rating lower than the wall** it penetrates.
- **Fire-smoke damper specified but wired to nothing**, so it never closes on
  alarm.

## Standards referenced

Compartmentation and services provisions under **NBC 2016, Part 4**. Fire
damper requirements under **EN 15650** and **UL 555**.
