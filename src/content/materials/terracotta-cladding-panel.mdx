## What it is

**Extruded and fired clay**, produced as hollow-section rainscreen panels or as
solid baguette rods. Panels hang on horizontal aluminium rails within a
ventilated cavity; baguettes clip into vertical or horizontal carriers in front
of the facade line.

The colour comes from the clay body and the firing, not from a coating.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Extruded hollow panel, or solid / hollow baguette |
| Panel thickness | 18mm — 30mm typical for hollow sections |
| Fire class | A1, non-combustible, to EN 13501-1 |
| Finish | Natural, engobe, or glazed |
| Fixing | Aluminium rail with clips; concealed |
| Water absorption | Low; specified by product to EN 1304 family methods |

## Why it gets specified

**Permanent colour.** A fired clay body does not chalk or fade. On buildings
where facade access for recoating is difficult or expensive, this is the
strongest argument for the material.

**Genuine depth.** The extruded section gives a shadow line and surface relief
that flat panels reproduce only graphically.

**Solar control without losing the view.** Baguette arrays cut direct gain on
east and west elevations while remaining visually open — relevant on Indian
elevations where the sun is low enough that overhangs do little.

## What drives the cost

Panel format and section depth · finish, with glazed costing more than natural
or engobe · panel size and the number of special shapes such as corners,
reveals and closers · rail and clip system · baguette length and support
spacing · handling and breakage allowance · building height and access.

Terracotta sits **above ACP and HPL**, and typically **below natural stone**.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Support spacing set by appearance.** Baguette spans and panel rail spacing
  follow from the wind pressure for that building under IS 875 Part 3, not from
  what looks even on an elevation drawing.
- **No breakage allowance.** Fired clay chips at corners in transit and
  handling. Order and programme should assume it.
- **Special shapes ordered late.** Corners, reveals and closers are extruded to
  order with their own lead time; leaving them to site measurement delays the
  whole elevation.
- **Cavity barriers omitted** because the panel is non-combustible. The panel
  is not the assembly.

## Standards referenced

Reaction-to-fire classification to **EN 13501-1**. Clay product requirements
in the **EN 1304** family. Wind loading on cladding and its supports to
**IS 875 (Part 3)**.
