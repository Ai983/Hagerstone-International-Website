## What it is

A **single sheet of aluminium alloy**, folded into a cassette and hung on a
sub-frame. Unlike ACP there is no polymer core and no bonding line — the panel
is one material through its whole thickness.

That single fact drives most of the specification decisions around it.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Alloy | 3003, 3105 or 5005 to IS 737 |
| Thickness | 2mm — 4mm for cassette cladding |
| Finish | PVDF coil coating, or anodised |
| Coating standard | AAMA 2605 for the highest weathering class |
| Fire classification | A1 / non-combustible to EN 13501-1 |
| Panel format | Cassette with folded returns, rear stiffeners as required |

## Why it gets specified

**Fire performance is not a discussion.** The panel is metal through its
section, so there is no core grade to argue about, no test certificate to
match against a delivered batch, and no substitution risk at supply stage. On
high-rise, healthcare and high-occupancy buildings this removes the single
most contested item in a facade submission.

**It holds a fold.** Deep returns, sharp corners, tapered panels and curved
geometry are achievable in solid sheet in ways a composite panel resists,
because there is no core to delaminate at a tight radius.

**Flatness.** On a prestige elevation viewed in raking light, a well-detailed
solid aluminium cassette with rear stiffeners reads flatter than most
alternatives.

## What drives the cost

Sheet thickness · alloy · finish class, with PVDF and anodised costing more
than polyester · panel size and the number of folds · rear stiffener extent ·
whether panels are flat, curved or tapered · sub-frame complexity · building
height and access.

Solid aluminium typically lands **above ACP and below** most natural stone
per square metre, but the gap narrows once ACP is specified in A2 grade.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Thin sheet on a large panel.** The result is oil-canning — visible waviness
  that no amount of site adjustment removes. It is a design error, fixed at
  panel-size and stiffener stage, not on site.
- **Stiffeners bonded with the wrong adhesive.** A facade in Indian summer sun
  runs far hotter than ambient. The bond must be rated for that service
  temperature or stiffeners debond and the panel bellies out.
- **Assuming a non-combustible panel makes a compliant facade.** It does not.
  Cavity barriers and insulation grade still govern.
- **Leaving the finish class unnamed.** "Powder coated" spans products lasting
  five years and products lasting twenty-five. Name the resin class.

## Standards referenced

Sheet alloy and temper under **IS 737**. Reaction-to-fire classification to
**EN 13501-1**. High-performance organic coating performance to **AAMA 2605**.
