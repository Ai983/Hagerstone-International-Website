## What it is

Float glass carrying a **metallic or metal-oxide coating** that reflects part
of the solar spectrum. It is the primary tool for meeting an SHGC target on a
glazed Indian facade.

## Specification at a glance

| Property | Typical specification |
| --- | --- |
| Coating type | Pyrolytic (hard) or sputtered (soft) |
| Standard | EN 1096, class stated |
| Coating surface | Named by number, typically surface 2 |
| Light transmission | Stated as a percentage |
| Solar factor | SHGC value stated for the build-up |
| Use | Soft coat only inside a sealed DGU |

## Hard coat against soft coat

| | Pyrolytic (hard) | Sputtered (soft) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Durability | Can face outward, handled normally | Fragile; sealed cavity only |
| Thermal performance | Moderate | Substantially better |
| Toughening | Straightforward | Needs heat-treatable grade |
| Single glazing | Possible | Not suitable |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |

## What to state on the order

The coating **name and manufacturer**, the **surface number**, the **glass
thickness and build-up**, the **light transmission**, the **SHGC for the
complete unit** — not the monolithic pane — and whether the glass is
heat-strengthened or toughened.

A coating performance figure quoted for a single pane does not describe the
double-glazed unit that will be installed.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Surface number unstated**, so the unit is assembled with the coating in the
  wrong position and the performance calculation no longer applies.
- **Soft coat exposed** on a single-glazed application. It degrades.
- **SHGC quoted monolithic**, then used in an ECBC compliance calculation.
- **Reflectance chosen purely visually**, without considering glare onto
  neighbouring buildings and roads.
- **Mixed batches across one elevation.** Coating colour varies subtly between
  production runs and shows on a continuous facade.

## Standards referenced

Coated glass classification under **EN 1096**. Fenestration SHGC and U-value
targets under **ECBC 2017**. Architectural safety glass to **IS 2553 (Part 1)**.
