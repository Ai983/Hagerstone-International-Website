## What it is

**Unplasticized polyvinyl chloride pipe** — rigid, corrosion-free and
inexpensive. Used for cold water supply under pressure and, in its SWR grades,
for soil, waste and rainwater drainage.

## Specification at a glance

| Application | Standard | Grades |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold water pressure | IS 4985 | Pressure classes by working pressure |
| Soil and waste | IS 13592 | Type B, thicker wall |
| Ventilation and rainwater | IS 13592 | Type A, thinner wall |
| Max service temperature | — | Approximately 60°C |
| Jointing | — | Solvent cement, or ring seal |

## Pressure pipe against drainage pipe

These are different products and the distinction gets blurred on site:

- **Pressure pipe to IS 4985** is classified by working pressure and is what a
  cold water supply main needs.
- **SWR pipe to IS 13592** is for gravity flow only. It has no meaningful
  pressure rating.

Putting SWR pipe on a pressurised line, or a thin Type A on a soil stack, are
both substitutions that look identical once the shaft is closed.

## Drainage detailing that matters

**Gradient.** Too flat and solids settle; too steep and water outruns the
solids, leaving them behind. IS 12251 sets the practice.

**Ventilation.** An unvented stack siphons traps dry and lets drain odour into
occupied space — a very common complaint in Indian buildings, and almost always
a venting omission.

**Access.** Cleanouts at direction changes and at intervals on long runs, sized
and positioned so they can actually be reached after the ceiling is closed.

## Where projects go wrong

- **UPVC on hot water.** It will fail.
- **Type A used for soil.** Thinner wall, wrong duty.
- **Long solvent-welded runs with no expansion allowance**, so the pipe cracks
  at a joint after a few hot seasons.
- **Support spacing copied from metal practice.** UPVC sags.
- **Stored in sun.** UV degrades the pipe before it is ever installed.

## Standards referenced

Pressure pipe under **IS 4985**. Soil and waste discharge systems under
**IS 13592**. Building drainage practice under **IS 12251**.
