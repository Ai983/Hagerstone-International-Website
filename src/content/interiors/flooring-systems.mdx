## What we deliver

**Carpet tile, LVT, vinyl, stone, wood and raised access flooring** — selected
per zone rather than applied uniformly, and laid on a subfloor that has been
checked rather than assumed.

Flooring is the largest continuous surface in an office. It sets how the space
sounds, how it wears, and how much of it has to be lifted the next time the
layout changes.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Laying on a slab that has not dried.** The most common cause of vinyl and
  LVT failure, and it shows up weeks after handover.
- **Skipping the level survey.** Every dip telegraphs through a thin floor
  finish, and a raised floor over an uneven slab rocks.
- **No attic stock.** Carpet tile's main advantage is single-tile replacement,
  which needs matching stock from the same batch.
- **Hard flooring everywhere.** The office is then permanently loud, and the
  fix afterwards is expensive.
- **Transitions left to site.** Thresholds between two materials are visible,
  trip-prone and worth detailing on a drawing.
