## What we deliver

**Glazed office partitions, frameless doors and acoustic glazed rooms** —
specified around the privacy each room actually needs rather than a single
default applied across the floor.

Glass is what makes a modern office feel open while still being divided. It is
also where acoustic expectations quietly break: clients specify glass for
daylight and openness, then expect the confidentiality of a solid wall.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Glass specified for openness, judged on privacy.** Decide per room what it
  actually has to do before choosing the build-up.
- **Perimeter sealing ignored.** Sound over the head track through the shared
  plenum defeats even good glazing.
- **Hardware chosen after the glass is ordered.** Cut-outs are made before
  toughening; a late change means new glass.
- **Manifestation forgotten.** Full-height clear glass in a circulation route is
  a walk-into hazard and needs visible marking.
- **Floor spring pocket formed badly.** The most common cause of frameless doors
  that sag, bind or stop closing.
