## What we deliver

**CCTV, access control, public address and voice evacuation, intercom, and
building management interfaces** — designed with the building services and
installed with the fit-out rather than bolted on afterwards.

Low voltage systems are small in cost relative to the building and large in how
they affect daily use and safety. They are also the systems most often left to
the end, which is why they so often end up in surface conduit across a finished
ceiling.

| Access control | Readers, locks, door release, controller, fire interface |
| Public address | Speakers, zoning, voice evacuation interface |
| Intercom | Reception and entry intercom |
| BMS interfaces | Integration points for HVAC, lighting and alarms |
| Containment | Dedicated ELV trays and conduits, rack space |
| Testing | Coverage check, interface tests, commissioning |

## Where projects go wrong

- **Access-controlled escape doors not released on alarm.** A genuine safety
  failure, not a technical detail.
- **Camera positions chosen without a coverage review.** Blind spots at exactly
  the entrances and corridors that matter.
- **ELV added after the ceiling closes.** Cut ceilings and surface containment.
- **No dedicated containment.** ELV sharing trays with power invites
  interference and confusion at every future change.
- **Rack space not planned.** Equipment ends up in a cupboard with no
  ventilation.
