## What we deliver

**Cold and hot water supply, drainage and venting, sanitary fixtures, and
pumping** for offices, hotels, hospitals, factories and institutional buildings —
installed, pressure tested and handed over.

Plumbing is the service nobody notices until it goes wrong, and when it does, it
is usually concealed inside a finished floor or wall. The work that prevents that
happens at installation: falls, venting, supports and testing.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Insufficient drainage fall.** Slow drainage and blockages, discovered after
  the floor is finished.
- **Venting omitted or undersized.** Traps siphon, and the smell arrives in the
  occupied space.
- **Pressure testing skipped.** Leaks are then found in finished ceilings.
- **Risers located late on a fit-out.** Wet areas end up where drainage can
  reach, not where the layout needed them.
- **Incompatible pipe materials at joints.** A frequent and avoidable cause of
  failure.
