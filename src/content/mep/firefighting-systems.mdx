## What we deliver

**Fire hydrant and hose reel systems, wet risers, automatic sprinklers, fire pump
rooms and the associated piping** — installed, pressure tested and commissioned.

This is life-safety work, and the standard it is held to is correspondingly
higher. A sprinkler system is tested once properly and then, with luck, never
used — which means whatever was wrong with it on day one is discovered on the
worst day of the building's life.

## What drives the cost

Hazard classification · building height and floor area · whether sprinklers are
required in addition to hydrants · pump capacity and the diesel standby · tank
volume · pipe material and lengths · the number of enclosures on a fit-out, since
each needs its own coverage.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Pump room space allocated before the calculation.** The pumps and valves
  then do not fit, and the room is rebuilt.
- **Sprinklers not reassessed after partitioning.** Every enclosure needs its
  own coverage.
- **Pressure testing rushed or skipped.** The test is the only proof the system
  holds pressure; leaks found later are found in finished ceilings.
- **Obstructions ignored.** Ducts, beams and cable trays that block a sprinkler's
  spray pattern leave areas uncovered despite heads being present.
- **No maintenance plan.** Jockey pumps, valves and diesel engines need routine
  testing to still work when called on.
