    answer: "Conventional systems report a fire by zone — you know which area, not which device.     answer: "Smoke detectors in most occupied areas, because they respond earliest. Heat detectors where smoke detectors would false-alarm — kitchens, pantries, some plant rooms, dusty areas. ## What we deliver

**Fire alarm panels, smoke and heat detection, manual call points, sounders and
hooters, and interfaces** to firefighting, HVAC shutdown, lifts and public
address — installed, programmed and tested.

Detection is the part of fire safety that buys time. Suppression limits the fire;
detection is what gets people out before it matters. That makes coverage and
reliability the whole job — and a system that false-alarms constantly fails at
it too, because people stop responding.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Wrong detector type for the space.** The leading cause of nuisance alarms.
- **Coverage not reassessed after partitioning.** New rooms, and walls to slab,
  leave gaps.
- **Cause-and-effect never tested end to end.** The panel alarms, but the AHU
  does not shut down and the lifts do not home.
- **Detectors in the ceiling void forgotten.** Deep plenums with services can
  require detection above the ceiling as well as below.
- **No maintenance regime.** Detectors need periodic testing and cleaning to
  stay reliable.
