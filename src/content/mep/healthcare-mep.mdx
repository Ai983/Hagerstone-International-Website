## What we deliver

**Interiors and MEP for hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres** — across
clinical, critical and public areas, with the services each zone actually
requires.

Healthcare is where building services stop being about comfort and start being
about continuity. The design question is not only whether a system works, but
what happens to the patient in the room when it stops.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Treating a hospital like a large office.** The redundancy and zoning
  requirements are fundamentally different.
- **Critical areas without continuity of supply.** Power and air to OT and ICU
  must survive a single failure.
- **Pressure relationships between zones not maintained.** Air moving the wrong
  way between clean and dirty areas defeats infection control.
- **Live-facility work without infection-control barriers.** Construction dust
  in a clinical area is a patient-safety issue.
- **Equipment requirements confirmed late.** Imaging and theatre equipment bring
  specific power, cooling and structural needs that must be designed in.
