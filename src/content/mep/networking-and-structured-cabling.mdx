## What we deliver

**Structured cabling, network racks, patch panels, data outlets and
containment** — laid out against the desk plan, installed with the fit-out, and
tested before handover.

Network cabling is the one piece of an office's IT that lives inside the
building. Equipment gets replaced every few years; the cable in the ceiling is
expected to last the fit-out. Getting its quantity, category and routes right is
far cheaper at installation than at any point after.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Points planned exactly to today's headcount.** Every addition later is a new
  run through a finished ceiling.
- **No certification.** Intermittent faults that are close to impossible to
  trace afterwards.
- **Data sharing containment with power.** Interference and confusion at every
  future change.
- **Rack room without ventilation or power planning.** Heat shortens equipment
  life.
- **No labelling.** The next person to touch the network spends hours tracing
  cables.
