## What we deliver

**Commercial buildings from groundwork to handover** — foundations, structure,
masonry, finishes, facade, MEP and interiors delivered as a single turnkey scope.

Hagerstone describes this work as *from structure to splendour*: the same
organisation takes a building from its foundations to a finished interior. That
is what makes speed possible — the programme is planned end to end rather than
stitched together from separate contracts.

## Scope of works

| Stage | What we deliver |
| --- | --- |
| Planning | Programme, sequencing, procurement plan |
| Groundwork | Excavation, foundations, plinth |
| Structure | RCC frame or PEB, slabs, staircases |
| Masonry & finishes | Blockwork, plaster, flooring, painting |
| Envelope | Facade, glazing, cladding, doors and windows |
| Services | Electrical, HVAC, plumbing, firefighting, ELV |
| Interiors | Fit-out, joinery, furniture |
| Handover | Testing, snagging, documentation |

## What drives the cost

Building size and height · structural system — RCC or PEB · foundation type,
which depends on the soil · facade specification · MEP complexity · interior
specification · programme compression · site access and logistics.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Design not frozen before construction.** Every change during construction
  costs more than the same change on paper.
- **Soil conditions assumed.** Foundation design depends on a proper soil
  investigation.
- **Trades contracted separately.** The interfaces between them are where time
  and money are lost.
- **Long-lead items ordered late.** Facade, lifts and major MEP plant can govern
  the programme.
- **Approvals not in the programme.** Statutory clearances sit outside the site's
  control.
