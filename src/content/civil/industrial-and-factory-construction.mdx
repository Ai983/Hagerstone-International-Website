## What we deliver

**Factories, production plants, warehouses, admin blocks, canteens and
industrial facilities** — civil structure, PEB sheds, MEP, firefighting and
office interiors delivered as one project.

An industrial client needs a building that serves a process and starts operating
on time. That makes two things critical: designing around the process rather than
around a generic shed, and delivering the structure, the services and the offices
without the gaps that separate contracts create.

## Scope of works

| Element | What we deliver |
| --- | --- |
| Production building | PEB or RCC, designed around the process layout |
| Floors | Grade slabs designed for machinery and racking loads |
| Admin block | RCC structure, facade and office interiors |
| Utilities | Electrical including HT/LT, plumbing, drainage |
| Fire protection | Hydrants, sprinklers, detection, pump room |
| Ventilation | Natural and mechanical ventilation, HVAC where needed |
| Canteen & amenities | Staff facilities and washrooms |
| External works | Roads, drainage, boundary as in scope |

## Where projects go wrong

- **Building designed before the process layout.** The most expensive mistake in
  industrial construction.
- **Floor loads underestimated.** Machinery and racking concentrate heavy loads.
- **Utilities sized for day one.** Production grows, and upgrading power and
  water later is costly.
- **Fire protection an afterthought.** It shapes water storage, pump rooms and
  roof heights.
- **Office block and plant as separate projects.** Two sets of foundations,
  services and approvals on one plot.
