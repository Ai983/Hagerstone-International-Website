## What we deliver

**Foundations, RCC frames, slabs, staircases, and structural works** for
commercial and industrial buildings — from groundwork upwards, with testing at
every stage.

Structure is the part of a building that is invisible once finished and
impossible to correct afterwards. The quality of what gets poured, compacted and
cured decides the building's life far more than anything added later.

## Where projects go wrong

- **No soil investigation.** The foundation is then designed on assumption.
- **Insufficient curing.** In Indian heat, concrete that dries too fast never
  reaches design strength.
- **Poor compaction.** Voids and honeycombing weaken the member.
- **Inadequate cover.** Reinforcement too close to the surface corrodes.
- **Service openings not planned.** Coring through a finished beam or slab later
  is risky and expensive.
