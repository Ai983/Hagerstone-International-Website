## Why it matters

Every drywall change is a demolition. For companies that reorganise often,
demountable systems turn that into a relocation, reducing downtime, waste and
the cost of the next change.

## How it works in practice

| | Drywall | Demountable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial cost | Lower | Higher |
| Reconfiguration | Demolish and rebuild | Dismantle and reinstall |
| Waste | High on change | Low |
| Finish | Painted board | Factory-finished panels, glazing |
| Best for | Stable layouts | Changing layouts |

## Common mistakes

- **Choosing it for a layout that never changes.** The premium is not recovered.
- **Poor sealing at the head.** Sound travels through the plenum.
- **Crossing fire compartment lines** with a system not rated for it.
