## Why it matters on a facade project

A facade is the one package where the cost of being wrong scales with the whole
building. An error in a shop drawing does not appear once — it appears in every
panel, and by the time it is visible the panels are installed at height on a
sealed elevation.

The mock-up exists to move that discovery forward, onto a few square metres, at
a point where changing the answer is still cheap.

## What gets tested, in order

| Stage | Test | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Visual approval | Colour, tint, joint width, spandrel-to-vision match in daylight |
| 2 | Air infiltration — ASTM E283 | Leakage rate at design pressure |
| 3 | Static water — ASTM E331 | No penetration under sustained spray and pressure |
| 4 | Dynamic water — AAMA 501.1 | Performance under wind-driven rain |
| 5 | Structural loading | Deflection under positive and negative design pressure |
| 6 | Re-test after structural | Seals still perform after the frame has flexed |

The final step is the one most often dropped, and the most revealing. A facade
that passes water testing before structural loading, then fails after it, is
telling you the seals cannot survive the movement the building will actually put
them through.

## Common mistakes

- **Building the mock-up from different components.** A mock-up assembled with
  substituted gaskets or a different sealant proves nothing about what will be
  installed.
- **Treating it as a formality after production has started.** If the factory is
  already running, the mock-up has no authority and is only theatre.
- **Approving colour under artificial light.** Vision and spandrel glass read
  differently in daylight, at angle, with a lit interior behind one and a dark
  cavity behind the other.
- **Skipping the post-structural water re-test.** Movement is what opens seals.
- **Testing at the wrong pressure.** Test pressure derives from the design wind
  pressure for that building under IS 875 Part 3, not from a standard default.

## Standards referenced

Air leakage is measured to **ASTM E283**, static water penetration to **ASTM
E331**, and dynamic water penetration to **AAMA 501.1**. Test pressures derive
from the design wind pressure established under **IS 875 (Part 3)**.
