## Why it matters

Glass walls along corridors, stairs and lobbies often sit on fire compartment
lines. There, ordinary glass would fail within minutes; fire-rated glazing keeps
the compartment intact long enough for people to escape.

## How it works in practice

| Rating | Stops flames & smoke | Limits heat | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E (integrity) | Yes | No | Where people do not pass close by |
| EI (insulation) | Yes | Yes | Escape routes, adjacent occupied areas |

## Common mistakes

- **Rated glass in an unrated frame.** The system, not the glass, carries the
  rating.
- **E where EI is needed.** Heat on escape routes can be dangerous.
- **Unrated penetrations** through fire-rated glass walls.
