## Why it matters

Glass is usually the weakest part of the envelope thermally. A low-E coating
changes that without making the glass dark, which is why it is central to
meeting ECBC targets while keeping daylight.

## How it works in practice

| Type | How applied | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft-coat | Vacuum deposition after manufacture | Inside DGUs, best performance |
| Hard-coat | Fused during manufacture | Single glazing, durable |
| Solar-control low-E | Soft-coat tuned to block solar heat | Hot climates |

## Common mistakes

- **Using soft-coat in single glazing.** The coating is exposed and degrades.
- **Coating on the wrong surface.** Performance changes significantly.
- **Choosing on appearance only.** Check SHGC, U-value and light transmission.

## Standards referenced

Fenestration performance under **ECBC 2017**. Safety glass under **IS 2553
(Part 1)**.
