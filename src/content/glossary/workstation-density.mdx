## Why it matters

Density is the number that turns a floor plate into a headcount — and a
headcount into a lease decision. Get it wrong and a company either pays for
space it cannot use or signs for a floor that cannot hold its team.

## How it works in practice

| Density (sq ft carpet / person) | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Below 60 | Very dense — call centres, back offices |
| 60 – 80 | Efficient corporate |
| 80 – 100 | Standard corporate with good support space |
| Above 100 | Premium or cabin-heavy offices |

## Common mistakes

- **Measuring on chargeable area.** Density must use carpet area.
- **Counting desks only.** Meeting rooms and support space are part of the total.
- **Ignoring egress.** Denser floors need more exit capacity.

## Standards referenced

Occupant load and exits under **NBC 2016, Part 4**.
