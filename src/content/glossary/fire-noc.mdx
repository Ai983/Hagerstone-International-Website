## Why it matters

Occupation, insurance and sometimes financing depend on the fire NOC. Because it
rests on both drawings and installed systems, fire protection has to be designed
correctly from the start — changing escape routes or pump rooms late is far
harder than getting them right on paper.

## How it works in practice

| Stage | Typical content |
| --- | --- |
| Provisional / design stage | Drawings of exits, separation, fire systems |
| Construction | Systems installed to the approved design |
| Final / occupancy stage | Inspection and testing of installed systems |
| Renewal | Periodic renewal where required by the authority |

## Common mistakes

- **Treating it as paperwork at the end.** It depends on design decisions made at
  the start.
- **Fit-out changes not reflected.** Re-partitioned floors can change escape
  routes and coverage.
- **Systems not maintained.** Renewals and inspections expect working systems.

## Standards referenced

Fire and life safety requirements under **NBC 2016, Part 4**, applied through
each state's fire service rules.
