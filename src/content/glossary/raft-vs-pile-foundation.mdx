## Why it matters

Foundations are the most expensive part of the building to get wrong, because
they cannot be corrected once the structure sits on them. The choice between
footings, raft and piles is a soil question — and answering it without a proper
investigation means guessing with the whole building.

## How it works in practice

| Foundation | Carries load | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isolated footing | Directly under each column | Good soil, moderate loads |
| Raft | Across the whole footprint | Heavy loads, moderate soil |
| Pile | Down to deeper strata | Weak surface soil, heavy loads |

## Common mistakes

- **No soil investigation.** The foundation is then designed on assumption.
- **Copying the neighbouring building's foundation.** Soil varies across short
  distances.
- **Ignoring the water table.** It affects excavation, waterproofing and design.

## Standards referenced

Raft foundations under **IS 2950 (Part 1)**, pile foundations under **IS 2911**,
and foundations in soils under **IS 1904**.
