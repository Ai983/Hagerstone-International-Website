## Why it matters

Whether a building is supplied at HT or LT changes the electrical room, the
capital budget, the approval route and the tariff. It is decided by the load,
and it needs to be known before the building layout fixes the space for a
substation.

## How it works in practice

| | HT panel | LT panel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voltage | Utility high voltage, e.g. 11kV | Low voltage, typically 415V |
| Position | Before the transformer | After the transformer |
| Serves | Transformer | Building distribution |
| Needed when | Load above the DISCOM's LT limit | Every building |

## Common mistakes

- **Planning no substation space.** HT supply needs room for the HT panel and
  transformer, with access and ventilation.
- **Underestimating load growth.** A building that crosses the HT threshold
  later faces an expensive conversion.
- **Treating the LT panel as the end of design.** Discrimination and
  sub-distribution matter as much as the main panel.

## Standards referenced

Installation safety under the **CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric
Supply) Regulations, 2023**. Wiring practice under **IS 732**.
