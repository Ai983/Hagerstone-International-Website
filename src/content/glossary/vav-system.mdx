## Why it matters

Comfort complaints in new offices are very often VAV complaints. The system is
good at zoned control, but only if its zones match the layout — which they
rarely do after a fit-out unless someone re-balances them.

## How it works in practice

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| AHU | Supplies conditioned air |
| VAV box | Varies airflow to one zone |
| Zone thermostat | Tells the box how much air is needed |
| BMS | Coordinates boxes and the AHU |

## Common mistakes

- **Not re-balancing after a fit-out.** The most common cause of hot and cold
  rooms.
- **One box serving rooms with different loads.** Neither room is comfortable.
- **Blocking the return path.** Walls to slab can starve the system of return air.
