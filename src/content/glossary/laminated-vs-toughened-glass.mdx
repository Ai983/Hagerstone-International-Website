## Why it matters

Glass specification is a safety decision. The question is not only how strong
the glass is, but what happens after it breaks — and the answer determines where
each type can legally and sensibly be used.

## How it works in practice

| | Toughened | Laminated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | High | Similar to ordinary unless toughened |
| Breakage | Small granules | Fragments held by interlayer |
| After breaking | Pane falls out | Pane stays in place |
| Typical use | Doors, partitions, facades | Overhead, balustrades, floors |

## Common mistakes

- **Monolithic toughened glass overhead.** It falls when it breaks.
- **Cutting toughened glass after treatment.** It cannot be cut or drilled.
- **Ignoring laminated for barriers.** An open edge at height is dangerous.
