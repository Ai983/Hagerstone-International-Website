## Why it matters

The ceiling is the largest surface in most offices. Its type decides how the
space sounds, how it looks, and how easily the services above it can be reached
for years to come.

## How it works in practice

| | Gypsum | Mineral fibre grid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appearance | Seamless | Visible grid |
| Access | Via access panels | Lift any tile |
| Acoustics | Low absorption unless perforated | Good absorption |
| Shapes | Curves, steps, bulkheads | Flat |
| Best for | Reception, corridors, features | Open plan, meeting rooms |

## Common mistakes

- **Gypsum over dense services.** Access becomes destructive.
- **Too few access panels.** Plan them at valves, dampers and VAV boxes.
- **Ignoring acoustics.** Plain plasterboard makes a loud open plan.

## Standards referenced

Gypsum plasterboard under **IS 2095**.
