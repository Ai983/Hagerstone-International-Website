## Why it matters

A concrete's strength is only partly set by its mix. The rest comes from what
happens in the following days. In Indian heat and dry winds, surfaces can lose
water within hours, and concrete that dries out early never reaches the
strength its cube results promised on paper.

## How it works in practice

| Method | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Ponding | Flat slabs |
| Wet hessian / gunny bags | Columns, beams, walls |
| Sprinkling | Large areas, regular watering |
| Plastic sheeting | Retaining moisture on slabs |
| Curing compound | Where water curing is impractical |

## Common mistakes

- **Stopping too early.** A few days of wetting is not the specified period.
- **Curing only the top surface.** Vertical faces dry fastest and are often
  neglected.
- **Removing formwork too soon.** Exposes young concrete before it has strength.
