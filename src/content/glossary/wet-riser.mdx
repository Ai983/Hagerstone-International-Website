## Why it matters

On a tall building, carrying hose up many floors wastes the minutes that matter.
A wet riser puts pressurised water at every floor, ready to connect, which is why
it is a standard provision on multi-storey buildings.

## How it works in practice

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Riser pipe | Vertical charged pipe through the building |
| Landing valves | Hose connections at each floor |
| Hose reels | First-aid firefighting for occupants |
| Fire pumps | Keep the riser pressurised |
| Fire service inlet | Allows the fire tender to boost supply |

## Common mistakes

- **Riser in an unprotected location.** Valves should be accessible from a
  protected area.
- **Pressure not tested.** Hydrostatic testing proves the system holds.
- **Valves obstructed.** Fit-outs sometimes block landing valve access.
