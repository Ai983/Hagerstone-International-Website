## Why it matters

LPD is where lighting design meets the energy code. A scheme can look fine on a
lux plot and still fail compliance if it uses too much power to get there.

## How it works in practice

| Factor | Effect on LPD |
| --- | --- |
| Fitting efficacy | Higher lumens per watt lowers LPD |
| Optics | Better light distribution needs fewer fittings |
| Target lux | Higher targets need more power |
| Controls | Reduce energy used, often credited by codes |

## Common mistakes

- **Adding fittings to fix dark spots.** Improve layout and optics first.
- **Over-lighting circulation.** Corridors do not need desk-level lux.
- **Ignoring daylight.** Perimeter zones can be dimmed for much of the day.

## Standards referenced

Lighting power density under **ECBC 2017**. Illumination levels under
**IS 3646**.
