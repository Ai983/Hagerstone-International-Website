## Why it matters

Every kilogram of facade and every gust of wind passes through the brackets. They
are small, hidden and repeated hundreds of times, and a design or installation
error in them repeats across the whole building.

## How it works in practice

| Function | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Dead load | Carries facade weight into the slab |
| Wind load | Resists positive and negative pressure |
| Adjustment | Up/down, in/out, side to side |
| Movement | Allows for thermal and structural movement |
| Isolation | Separates dissimilar metals to prevent corrosion |

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the slab-edge survey.** Tolerance problems appear only when panels
  arrive.
- **Rigid connections everywhere.** The facade must accommodate movement.
- **Dissimilar metals in contact.** Galvanic corrosion at hidden connections.

## Standards referenced

Wind loading under **IS 875 (Part 3)**. Steel design under **IS 800:2007**.
