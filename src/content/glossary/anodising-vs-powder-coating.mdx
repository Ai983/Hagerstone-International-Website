## Why it matters on a project

Finish is usually treated as an appearance decision and priced as a rate. It is
actually a durability decision that determines how the building looks in ten
years, and it is close to impossible to correct once the facade is installed —
recoating aluminium in situ never matches factory quality.

## How they compare

| | Anodising | Powder coating |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature of finish | Oxide layer grown from the metal | Polymer film applied on top |
| Can it peel? | No — it is part of the substrate | Yes, if pre-treatment was poor |
| Colour range | Limited: silver, bronze, champagne, black | Effectively any RAL shade |
| Colour consistency | Batch variation is real and visible | Excellent, consistent between batches |
| Abrasion resistance | Very high | Moderate |
| UV / chalking | Does not chalk | Depends entirely on resin grade |
| Coastal performance | Strong | Strong with PVDF, weak with polyester |
| Typical relative cost | Higher | Lower to comparable at PVDF grade |
| Repair on site | Very difficult | Touch-up possible, rarely invisible |

## Common mistakes

- **Accepting "powder coated" without naming the resin.** Standard polyester and
  PVDF to AAMA 2605 are different products with very different lifespans. The
  specification must name the grade, not the process.
- **Not batching anodised profiles.** Anodised colour varies between production
  runs. Profiles for one elevation should come from one batch, and that has to be
  planned before ordering.
- **Ignoring pre-treatment on powder coating.** Adhesion is entirely dependent on
  chromate or chrome-free pre-treatment. Coating over poorly prepared aluminium
  fails at the edges within a few years.
- **Choosing on rate alone.** The cheaper finish that fades in five years costs
  vastly more than the difference, because correcting it means access equipment
  across a live building.
- **Forgetting the fixings.** Stainless fasteners against a coated surface, and
  dissimilar metals in contact, cause staining and galvanic problems that are
  blamed on the finish.

## Standards referenced

Anodic coating thickness and quality classes follow **IS 1868**. High-performance
organic coatings — the PVDF class — are specified to **AAMA 2605**, which is the
grade to name when weathering and colour retention matter.
