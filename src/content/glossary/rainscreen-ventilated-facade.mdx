## Why it matters on a facade project

Ventilated rainscreen is the default for stone, terracotta, HPL, fibre-cement
and metal cladding on Indian commercial buildings — and it works on a principle
that surprises people the first time they hear it: **the outer skin is not
waterproof, and is not meant to be.**

Understanding that changes how the system gets detailed. The panel is a screen.
The membrane behind it is the weather line. Most rainscreen failures come from
teams treating the panel as the barrier and the membrane as an afterthought.

## How it is specified in practice

| Layer | What it does | Typical detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer panel | Sheds rain, UV, appearance | ACP, solid aluminium, HPL, stone, terracotta |
| Cavity | Drains and ventilates | 25-50mm, open at bottom and top |
| Cavity barrier | Stops fire and smoke spread | At every compartment line and around openings |
| Insulation | Thermal performance | Mineral wool preferred; non-combustible |
| Breather membrane | The actual weather line | Continuous, correctly lapped, taped |
| Sub-frame | Carries panel load to structure | Aluminium or MS brackets, thermally broken |
| Substrate | Structural backing | RCC, blockwork or sheathing board |

## Common mistakes

- **Omitting or shortcutting cavity barriers.** The cavity is continuous by
  design. Without barriers at each compartment line it becomes a route for fire
  and smoke, which is the exact mechanism that made cladding fires catastrophic.
- **Treating the membrane as secondary.** If it is discontinuous at a corner, a
  penetration or a lap, the building will leak regardless of panel quality.
- **Blocking the ventilation path.** Insulation pushed into the cavity or
  sealed-off openings stop the airflow, and the cavity stops working.
- **Thermal bridging at the brackets.** Hundreds of metal brackets through the
  insulation can undo the assembly's U-value. Thermally broken brackets or
  isolation pads matter more than most specifications assume.
- **Combustible insulation behind a combustible panel.** Each may pass in
  isolation; together in a ventilated cavity they are a different proposition.

of external wall assemblies, follows **NBC 2016, Part 4**. Opaque assembly
U-value is assessed under **ECBC 2017**.
