## Why it matters

Sanctioned load is set once and then shapes years of bills. Too low, and the
building pays penalties and faces an upgrade application. Too high, and it pays
fixed charges for capacity it never uses — and may have bought a larger
transformer than it needed.

## How it works in practice

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Connected load | Sum of all installed equipment ratings |
| Diversity | Allowance for equipment not running together |
| Maximum demand | Highest load actually drawn |
| Sanctioned load / contract demand | What the DISCOM approves |

## Common mistakes

- **Applying for connected load without diversity.** Leads to paying for
  capacity that is never used.
- **Ignoring future growth.** Load enhancement later is slower and costlier.
- **Leaving it to the end.** The application sits outside the site programme and
  can hold up energisation.

## Standards referenced

Installation safety under the **CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric
Supply) Regulations, 2023**. Sanction procedures are set by each state's
electricity distribution company.
