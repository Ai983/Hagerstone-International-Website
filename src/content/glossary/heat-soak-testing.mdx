## Why it matters

Spontaneous breakage is rare, but on a tall facade or above a lobby a single
unexpected failure is a safety incident and a costly replacement at height. Heat
soaking moves most of that risk into the factory.

## How it works in practice

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Toughening | Glass heated and rapidly cooled |
| Heat soak | Held at elevated temperature for a defined time |
| Screening | Panes with harmful inclusions break in the oven |
| Result | Lower in-service spontaneous breakage risk |

## Common mistakes

- **Not specifying it at all** on high-risk locations.
- **Assuming it eliminates risk.** It reduces risk; it does not remove it.
- **No record of treatment.** Ask for evidence that panes were heat soaked.

## Standards referenced

Heat soaked toughened glass under **EN 14179**. Safety glass under **IS 2553
(Part 1)**.
