## Why it matters

Network equipment is replaced every few years; the cable is expected to last the
fit-out. Choosing its category is a decision about what the office can support a
decade from now.

## How it works in practice

| | Cat 6 | Cat 6A |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bandwidth | 250 MHz | 500 MHz |
| 10 Gbps distance | ~55 m | 100 m |
| Cable size | Thinner | Thicker |
| Best for | General desk points | APs, backbone, high PoE |

## Common mistakes

- **Sizing containment for Cat 6, then installing Cat 6A.** It does not fit.
- **Skipping certification.** A link that connects is not proven to perform.
- **Mixing categories in one channel.** The link performs at the lowest grade.

## Standards referenced

Generic cabling under **ISO/IEC 11801**. Commercial cabling under **TIA-568**.
