## What we deliver

**System and custom fenestration** — openable, sliding, casement and
tilt-and-turn aluminium doors and windows, plus uPVC where the application
suits it, for commercial, high-rise and premium residential projects.

We work on engineered systems rather than assembled parts. A window is a
performance product: profile, hardware, gasket, drainage and glass have to be
designed as one assembly, or nobody can honestly state what it will do.

## What drives the cost

Opening type and size · thermally broken versus plain profile · glass build-up
(single, DGU, laminated, low-E) · hardware grade and origin · acoustic
performance requirement · finish (anodised versus PVDF powder coat) · floor
height and access.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Mixing system components.** Substituting a gasket or hardware set
  invalidates the tested performance of the whole assembly.
- **Specifying good glass in a plain frame.** Paying for high-performance DGU
  then bridging the assembly with unbroken aluminium wastes the benefit.
- **Ignoring drainage.** Water sitting in the glazing rebate attacks the DGU
  secondary seal and causes early fogging in monsoon conditions.
- **Leaving finish grade unnamed.** "Powder coated" spans products that last
  five years and products that last twenty-five. Specify the resin class.
