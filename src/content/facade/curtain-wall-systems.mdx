## What we deliver

Curtain wall in all three configurations — **unitised, semi-unitised and
conventional stick** — engineered, fabricated and installed as one accountable
package rather than split between a designer, a fabricator and an installer.

The system choice is made on evidence at survey stage. Building height, panel
repetition, crane availability and programme all point to a different answer,
and the wrong choice is expensive to reverse once brackets are set.

## What drives the cost

Height and access strategy · panel repetition across the elevation · glass
specification (SHGC, U-value, DGU build-up) · framing depth set by wind pressure
· whether the facade is unitised or stick · corner and setting-out complexity ·
programme compression.

## Where projects go wrong

- **System chosen before the survey.** Slab edge tolerance and crane access
  frequently rule out the system that was assumed at concept.
- **Ordering before the mock-up is signed off.** Once production starts, a change
  means re-tooling.
- **Structural glazing bite copied from another project.** Wind pressure is
  building-specific; corner and upper-floor panels need more than the average.
- **Spandrel treated as an afterthought.** The cavity behind runs far hotter than
  the vision area and needs heat-strengthened glass and insulation behind it.
