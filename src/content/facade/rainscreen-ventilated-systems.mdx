## What we deliver

**Imported ventilated envelope systems** — the sub-frame, insulation, breather
membrane, cavity barriers and outer panel installed as one designed assembly.

The system works on a principle that surprises people the first time they hear
it: the outer skin is not waterproof and is not meant to be. It is a screen. The
drained, ventilated cavity behind it is what manages the water that gets past.

## The assembly, outside in

| Layer | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Outer panel | Sheds rain, handles UV, sets appearance |
| Cavity, 25-50mm | Drains and ventilates; open at bottom and top |
| Cavity barrier | Stops fire and smoke at every compartment line |
| Insulation | Thermal performance; mineral wool preferred |
| Breather membrane | The actual weather line — continuous and lapped |
| Sub-frame | Carries panel load to structure; thermally broken |
| Substrate | RCC, blockwork or sheathing board |

## What drives the cost

Panel material — ACP, solid aluminium, HPL, terracotta, stone · cavity depth ·
extent of cavity barriers · insulation grade and thickness to meet ECBC ·
sub-frame complexity and bracket spacing · whether brackets are thermally broken
· building height and access strategy.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Cavity barriers omitted or shortcut.** The most serious failure mode
  available in this system.
- **Membrane treated as secondary.** A discontinuity at a corner, penetration or
  lap leaks regardless of panel quality.
- **Ventilation path blocked.** Insulation pushed into the cavity or sealed
  openings stop the airflow, and the cavity stops working.
- **Thermal bridging ignored.** Hundreds of brackets through the insulation can
  undo the assembly U-value the specification was written to achieve.
- **Combustible insulation behind a combustible panel.** Each may pass alone;
  together in a ventilated cavity they are a different proposition.
