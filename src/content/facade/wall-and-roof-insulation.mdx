## How the materials compare

| | PIR panel | Rock wool panel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal performance | Higher per mm | Lower per mm |
| Fire behaviour | Combustible core, rated systems available | Mineral, non-combustible |
| Thickness for a given U-value | Thinner | Thicker |
| Acoustic performance | Moderate | Better |
| Typical use | General envelope area, tight build-ups | Fire-critical zones, compartment lines |
| Relative cost | Comparable, varies by grade | Comparable, varies by grade |

## What drives the cost

Target U-value under ECBC for the climate zone · core material and fire grade ·
panel thickness · whether the roof is new-build or overlay · span and support
spacing · sheet profile and finish · junction and flashing complexity.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Insulating the walls and neglecting the roof.** On low-rise industrial
  buildings the roof usually carries the larger share of the heat gain.
- **Ignoring the fire grade in a ventilated cavity.** Combustible insulation
  behind a combustible panel with a continuous cavity is the assembly that has
  caused the worst facade fires.
- **Thermal bridging at fixings and brackets.** Continuous metal through the
  insulation can undo a significant share of the specified performance.
- **Poor junction detailing.** Corners, parapets, penetrations and eaves are
  where insulation continuity is broken, and where condensation appears.
