## What we deliver

**ACP, solid aluminium, other metal, tile and stone cladding** — engineered
sub-frame, panel fabrication and installation, with the cavity, barriers and
weather line detailed as part of the assembly rather than left to the site team.

Since Grenfell, cladding fire performance is the first question in any fire
review. The panels look identical from outside; what separates a compliant
facade from a dangerous one is the core material and what happens in the cavity
behind.

Core grade (PE / FR / A2) · panel material — ACP versus solid aluminium versus
stone · finish class, with PVDF costing more than polyester · sub-frame
complexity and cantilever · cavity depth and barrier extent · panel size and
wastage · building height and access.

## Where projects go wrong

- **"FR ACP" with nothing else specified.** FR is a family, not a grade. Without
  the EN 13501-1 class and the certificate, the specification is unenforceable.
- **No cavity barriers.** The single most serious omission in ventilated
  cladding, and the mechanism behind catastrophic facade fires.
- **Substitution at supply stage.** The panel that arrives should be verifiable
  against the panel approved — by cut section and batch traceability.
- **Treating the membrane as secondary.** The breather membrane behind the panel
  is the actual weather line. A discontinuity there leaks regardless of panel
  quality.
- **Thermal bridging at brackets.** Hundreds of metal brackets through the
  insulation can undo the assembly's U-value.
