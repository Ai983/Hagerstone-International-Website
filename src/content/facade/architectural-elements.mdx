## What we deliver

**Canopies, balusters, pergolas and skylights** — bespoke architectural
elements fabricated in-house and coordinated with the surrounding envelope.

These are the elements that give a building its character at eye level, and the
ones most often left to be resolved late. They are also structurally awkward:
almost all of them project, cantilever or sit overhead, which puts them in the
load cases where facade work most often goes wrong.

| Element | The governing issue |
| --- | --- |

## What drives the cost

Span and cantilever length · glass build-up on skylights and glazed canopies ·
structural steel or aluminium framing · finish class — anodised or PVDF powder
coat · drainage complexity · geometry, since curved and faceted forms cost
substantially more than orthogonal.

## Where projects go wrong

- **Designed for downward load only.** Uplift usually governs on projecting
  elements, and it is the load case most often missed.
- **Monolithic toughened glass overhead.** If it fails, the fragments fall.
  Laminated is the only acceptable specification.
- **Drainage as an afterthought.** A canopy that sheds water onto the entrance
  steps is a slip hazard and an obvious defect.
- **Condensation ignored on skylights.** Warm humid interior air against cold
  glass produces water. It has to be collected, not left to drip.
- **Late structural coordination.** Cantilevered elements need fixings designed
  into the primary structure, not drilled in afterwards.
